# Research Methodology

## How Experts Were Selected

Criteria for inclusion:
- Must be actively publishing content in 2024-2025
- Must apply what they teach (practitioners, not just writers)
- Must have a clear focus on AI + SEO intersection
- Must have verifiable track record (clients, tools, or companies)

Not selected based on: Google search ranking or follower count alone.

## How Content Was Collected

**YouTube Transcripts**
- Tool: Tactiq.io free transcript extractor
- Format: Cleaned and summarized into key insights + quotes
- Covers: 6 videos from 2 primary experts

**LinkedIn Posts**
- Method: Manual collection from expert profiles
- Format: Original post content + key takeaways extracted
- Covers: 4 posts from 2 primary experts

## Signal Quality Assessment

Each piece of content was evaluated for:
1. Is this based on real data or just opinion?
2. Does the expert show their work (methodology, numbers)?
3. Is this applicable to B2B SaaS specifically?
4. Is this from 2024-2025 (current)?

## Why These 2 Experts Are Primary Sources

**Kevin Indig** — analyzed 500,000+ AI overview citations, 815,000 prompt-page pairs. Publishes data-backed insights weekly via Growth Memo.

**Aleyda Solis** — 100k+ LinkedIn followers, surveyed 200+ SEO professionals, created the most comprehensive AI search optimization checklist available. Active daily publisher.

The other 8 experts in sources.md are queued for Phase 2 collection.
