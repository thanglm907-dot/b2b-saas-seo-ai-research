## Post Content

Likely the easiest and cheapest way to check AI bots activity on your site: The Microsoft Clarity AI Visibility Dashboard

It gives you:
- Number of requests, share of traffic, unique pages requested, violations of robots.txt rules
- Access per bot operator, bot primary purpose, path requests
- Scrape vs referral ratio, and more.

If you haven't yet: Start using MS Clarity now for free!

## Key Takeaways

- **Microsoft Clarity** = free tool to monitor AI bot activity on your site
- Shows which AI bots are crawling (GPTBot, Googlebot-AI, etc.) and how often
- Reveals **robots.txt violations** — bots ignoring your crawl rules
- Breaks down **scrape vs referral ratio** — are bots extracting content or sending traffic?
- Completely **free** — no excuse not to set it up
- Critical for B2B SaaS: if AI bots can't crawl your site, you won't appear in LLM answers
- Connects directly to Aleyda's checklist item #2: validate AI bot crawlability
