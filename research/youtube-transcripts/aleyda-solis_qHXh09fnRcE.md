# SEO Reloaded: Adapting Old SEO Rules in the New AI World — Aleyda Solis

**Expert:** Aleyda Solis  
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qHXh09fnRcE  
**Published:** July 14, 2025  
**Source:** The Search Session (with Gianluca Fiorelli)  
**Topic:** AI search mindset, client communication, SEO fundamentals that still apply

---

## Key Insights Summary

1. **SEO fundamentals haven't changed — execution has** — "Sophisticated, mature SEO in 2025 has very high overlap with AI search in terms of concepts and principles. We need to open up our thoughts on how we target new behaviors."

2. **Chunk optimization = better incentive for what we already did** — "We're not looking to rank entire pages anymore. Now paragraphs/passages within those pages can be extracted for answer synthesis — for queries that might not even connect directly with the page's intent."

3. **Real-world example of AI traffic changing landing patterns** — Aleyda's own homepage (traditionally ranking for "SEO consultant") now gets AI referral traffic from queries like "best SEO newsletters" and "free way to learn SEO" — because she's cited as author of SEOFOMO and LearningSSEO.io.

4. **E-commerce PDPs getting informational traffic via AI** — "I'm seeing prompts generating traffic to PDPs for very informational queries for which they would have never ranked in traditional search."

5. **Google is protecting its ad revenue first** — "They need to validate well that their ad business still works with this new interface. They don't lose money — that's pretty much what I believe they're testing."

6. **The "traffic decoupling" problem** — Higher impressions (more AI engagement) + fewer clicks = existential threat for publishers/affiliates who depend on traffic for ad revenue. Smaller players have no licensing deals with LLMs unlike big brands.

7. **Don't reinvent the wheel with AI tools** — "I try to avoid reinventing the wheel. I use LLMs to identify patterns across data I've already analyzed. Tools like Screaming Frog with LLM API integrations are the right approach."

8. **Client communication = biggest SEO skill** — "It is as important HOW we communicate as WHAT we communicate. Don't frame issues as technical problems — frame them as business outcomes."

9. **Newer SEOs need to test and experiment** — Message to newer SEOs: "It's okay to feel uncertain. This is like 2006-2007 all over again — lots of unknowns, proactivity is needed. Create test sites, experiment."

## Key Quotes

On AI search overlap with traditional SEO: "It's not that we were not already doing it. But because of the way that the retrieval system works, we're going to focus it now in slightly different ways because of how the mechanisms work."

On client communication: "Everything should be backed by data. But the way this is explained shouldn't be focused on identified issues, but more on what solving these issues will achieve — the goal, the business outcome."

On testing mindset: "Go to your data. A lot of things might work in their own scenario, might not necessarily in yours. Be proactive to test, validate for yourself. Once you have the data that backs it, move forward."

On SEO fundamentals: "Think about the user, think about marketing, think about the revenue and the final outcome, and don't get fixated on the trees — see the whole wood instead."

On AI tool usage: "I have my own sheets with different prompts I reuse for analysis. The ideal is tools like Screaming Frog that already have LLM API integrations — rather than using LLMs as interfaces not taught to deal with these specific use cases."

## Tactical Notes

**How Aleyda starts with a new client:**
1. Understand business model, USP, audience, competitors, differentiation
2. Identify current constraints (technical, content, resources, campaigns)
3. Understand what "success" means to decision makers — speak their language
4. Use their existing platforms/tools (reduce friction)
5. Deliver recommendations framed as business outcomes, not technical issues

**Pilot project approach for reluctant clients:**
- Pick a less important product/service line
- Test AI search optimization (topical authority, chunk optimization, answer optimization)
- Build internal case study from results
- Then roll out to other areas

**AI usage in daily work:**
- Pattern recognition across already-analyzed data
- Existing tools with LLM integrations (Screaming Frog API)
- Reusable prompt sheets for recurring analysis tasks
- NOT replacing existing specialized tools (e.g. Keyword Insights for topic clusters)
