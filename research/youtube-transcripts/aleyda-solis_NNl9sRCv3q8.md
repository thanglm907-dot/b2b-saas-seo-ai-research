## Key Insights Summary

1. **AI traffic is growing fast but still small** — Enterprise clients seeing 1% → 4% AI traffic growth in one year. Converting well but still <5% of total.

2. **AI traffic lands on different pages** — In e-commerce/SaaS, 60% of AI referral traffic goes to detail/product pages (PDPs), NOT category/listing pages. AI skips aggregators.

3. **Help centers & forums are goldmines** — SaaS companies with siloed help centers in subdomains are getting disproportionate AI traffic. Integrate them into main domain.

4. **YouTube + Reddit = AI citation engine** — One SaaS client gets significant ChatGPT citations because they have strong YouTube community + Reddit conversations linking to their videos. LLMs train on YouTube transcripts.

5. **Don't start with tools — start with data** — "Validate and assess rather than start saying 'give me budget, let's buy a tool.'" Use GA4 + SimilarWeb first.

6. **Technical: AI bots ≠ Googlebot** — Different user agents, different crawl rules. Content blocked for spam reasons in traditional SEO might be valuable for LLMs (e.g. internal search results).

7. **Content is still #1** — "Content in your own platform and third-party as well. Not fluff content, not generic content just to piggyback on trending topics."

8. **Roadmap = assess → fix crawl → content gap → measure** — Don't try to do everything at once. Pick top 3 product lines, double down for 6 months, then show data.

## Practical Roadmap (from Aleyda)

**Step 1 — Audience behavior assessment**
- Which LLMs does your audience actually use?
- What prompts/queries are they using there vs traditional search?
- Tools: SimilarWeb, Profound, Cysri

**Step 2 — Technical crawl validation**
- Are AI bots blocked by CDN, firewall, or robots.txt?
- Is content rendered via client-side JavaScript? (AI bots can't see it)
- Monitor web server logs for AI bot activity

**Step 3 — Content gap analysis**
- What content exists for traditional SEO vs what AI platforms need?
- Focus on informational content that complements commercial pages
- Very likely NOT starting from scratch — just reprioritizing backlog

**Step 4 — Pilot projects**
- Pick top 3 topics/product lines where you have unique positioning
- Double down for 6 months
- Measure: direct traffic uplift, referral from AI platforms, attribution

**Step 5 — Monitor and stay flexible**
- Things shift fast — communicate this to decision makers
- Track trends, not individual static answers (highly personalized)
