## Key Insights Summary

1. **AI Overviews are the biggest shift** — Google now answers queries directly, reducing click-through to websites. This is not hype, it's a real structural change.

2. **2024 was peak traffic year** — Volume will drop but remaining traffic will be higher quality (people closer to purchase decision).

3. **Reddit & forums rising** — Google intentionally shows more human-generated content as a counterbalance to AI content flood.

4. **AI content pitfalls:**
   - Don't use lazy 1-sentence prompts — good prompts are 500–800 words
   - Don't publish too much too fast — grow pages 1–3% per week max
   - Always keep a human in the loop to edit AI output

5. **Future playbook = LLM visibility** — Companies must figure out how to appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini answers, not just Google.

**On biggest SEO trends:**
There's AI, right? That's a huge one. And it is real. It is not a hype. It's a real actual change affecting lots of businesses and the common theme really is uncertainty. Everybody wonders how is this going to affect us? Is this going to grow the pie? Is this going to shrink the pie? It certainly redefines the playing field with new rules.

AI in SEO mostly in the form of Google's AI overviews — these AI answers at the very top of the search results — they really change search in so far that they give the answer right away to people and mostly make it redundant to click through to websites. I would say that is the biggest change right now.

Reddit has now become probably one of the top three websites in terms of size in the US. And it's a symbol for Google wanting to show more content from humans in this AI transition phase, but also humans wanting more conversations and more answers from other actual humans.

**On AI content pitfalls:**
One is to be lazy and not making the AI content really good. The prompts that I develop with companies, they're typically 500 to 800 words long. They're really in-depth. They very much specify the different sections of the content and most importantly they're often paired with some sort of a data source from the company.

The second pitfall is to publish too much content too quickly. If you publish a thousand articles tomorrow generated with AI, Google will be very skeptical. If you grow your domain by 50% week over week in number of pages, that is often too much for Google to handle.

**On the future of SEO:**
Search is really becoming more of an engagement model. Traffic volume is going to drop tremendously and as a result the traffic that still arrives to websites is going to be much more qualified and of much higher value.

The big challenge then is going to be: if we have less activity on our website that we can control, how can we be visible in LLMs? That's going to be a new playbook — how can we insert ourselves into the set of choices that people have when they want to make a purchase, as early on as possible.
