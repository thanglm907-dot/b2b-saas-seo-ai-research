## Key Insights Summary

1. **LLMs expand search, not replace it** — 95% of ChatGPT users also use Google. ChatGPT has 5.8B visits vs Google's 83.8B. SEO is still vital.

2. **"Fan-out" query technique** — LLMs break one query into many sub-queries to synthesize a comprehensive answer. Content must cover topics from multiple angles, not just one keyword.

3. **Passage-level vs page-level** — AI pulls specific paragraphs/chunks from pages (not the whole page). Each paragraph should be self-contained and semantically tight — one idea per section.

4. **Mentions matter now, not just links** — AI systems weigh brand citations and references across forums, Reddit, digital PR — not only backlinks.

5. **Allow AI bots to crawl your site** — Many CDNs/hostings block AI crawlers by default. Must validate and unblock GPTBot, Googlebot-AI, etc.

6. **JavaScript is invisible to AI bots** — Unlike Googlebot, LLM crawlers cannot render JavaScript. Critical content must be in raw HTML.

7. **AI search still <5% of revenue** — Traditional search still 50%+. Integrate AI search optimization INTO existing SEO, not as a separate department.

8. **No official measurement tools yet** — No AI-equivalent of Google Search Console. Use: GA4 segmented, SimilarWeb AI reports, Profound, Cysri for tracking.

---

## 10-Step AI Search Optimization Checklist (from presentation)

1. **Research AI search audience behavior** — Identify which LLMs your audience uses and what prompts they use
2. **Optimize for crawlability** — Unblock AI bots in robots.txt, CDN, and firewall rules
3. **Fix JavaScript rendering** — Ensure content is in raw HTML, not client-side JS
4. **Build topical authority hubs** — Create complete content hubs covering the full customer journey
5. **Optimize for chunk-level retrieval** — One concept per paragraph, semantically tight, self-contained
6. **Optimize for answer synthesis** — Summarize first, then expand; plain factual tone; use schema markup
7. **Make content citation-worthy** — Show real expertise (EEAT), verifiable claims, author attribution
8. **Grow third-party authority** — Digital PR, Reddit presence, forum mentions, social citations
9. **Optimize for multimodal** — Image alt text, video captions, accessibility
10. **Monitor AI search performance** — Track AI traffic in GA4, use SimilarWeb, Profound, Cysri

## Key Quotes

On SEO vs GEO: "Good SEO is good GEO — but is that enough? We need additional optimization shifts to maximize visibility in AI search platforms."

On content strategy: "It is more important to create complete topical hubs that are able to fulfill and provide comprehensive answers that LLMs need. It's better that we control the narrative rather than making LLMs rely on third-party information."

On chunk optimization: "Each paragraph or section should be treated as a standalone snippet — semantically tight, self-contained, with one clear concept. Write for users. If you are a good writer and know how to structure content well, you should be good."

On links vs mentions: "You don't necessarily need a link pointing to your site. Just a mention already matters. Citations and references of your brand already have weight in AI systems."

On B2B websites: "I will always advise to own your brand because you have full control of your narrative. A website is also a main source of information for LLMs."

## Tools Mentioned

- **SimilarWeb** — AI traffic benchmark vs competitors, top prompts per page
- **Profound** — Keyword/topic popularity across LLMs by country
- **Cysri** — Brand visibility and citation sources in LLM answers
- **Looker Studio + Radian dashboard** — Free AI traffic analytics from GA4
- **Screaming Frog / Sitebulb** — Crawl audit for JavaScript dependency
- **Frase / Ahrefs AI Content Helper** — Content optimization and gap analysis
- **Sparktoro** — Where your audience hangs out online
- **Schema.org validator** — Structured data validation
